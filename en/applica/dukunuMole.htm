Dukunú Molê


A hybrid simulator to support a serious game for participative landscape planning in São Tomé

Pierre Bommel, Christophe Le Page, Anne Dray, Claude Garcia

This hybrid simulator can be used to support a Role-Playing Game (RpG) called "Dukunú Mole".

The design of the game was initially requested by the NGO "BIRDLIFE", with the following starting question:.

How to materialize an action plan that conciliates human needs with the protection of natural resources in the buffer zone of São Tomé?

The game was designed and developed with members of Birdlife in association with the LEAF spin-off, the ETH University and the UMR SENS of CIRAD.

Briefing

On an imaginary island (or territory), let's travel back in time (1980) and play until today, decade by decade.

Description of the territory

There are 4 zones: 

  • The town ( in gray) with two paved roads on either side.

  • An agricultural zone (yellow) for vegetable production

  • A secondary forest zone with low-value trees

  • A primary forest zone with valuable old trees

Who lives here?

4 communities (Green, Yellow, Red, Blue), each with 3 families and 3 dobras (D$).

Each community has 2 property titles.

All untitled plots are open access.

What are the communities' objectives?

At the end of the decade, each community must : 

  • have wood for domestic use.

  • be able to feed all its families.

  • If needs are not met, food and wood must be purchased from the market. 

  • Optionally, provide health and education for families, only if food and wood needs are met. 

How can we achieve these objectives?

Each family can only do one activity per turn.

An activity is represented by a card and a token located on a plot of land.

Let's see how each activity works:

Horticultural

Activity to be located on a plot in the yellow zone. No cost.

Production: 2 units which can be consumed or sold at the market (show market).

Cocoa

Activity to be placed only on a plot in the secondary forest zone, with at least one shade tree.

1 D$ needed to start purchasing seedlings and fertilizer.

Need to rent a truck and pay transport costs, because Cocoa cannot be eaten, only sold at the market.

Cocoa yields vary according to the cycle (yield: 1/3/2).

Gathering 

Gathering takes place in secondary or primary forests.

Gathering yields 1 unit of Buzio or honey

The gathered product can be eaten or sold at the market.

Hunting

Hunting in secondary or primary forests

(show data)

Hunting is consumed or sold.

Wood for domestic use

Cutting a single tree yields one unit of wood. There are no costs involved. It is not sold on the market, but is intended for domestic use only. 

Wood for commercial use

Logging all the trees in a plot yields timber units that are sold on the market. You have to hire a truck and remove all the trees from the plot. 

Working in the city

A family can work in town as a moto-taxi. This is a low-paying job = 1 D$. On the other hand, several families can do this activity in town. There is no activity card for this activity.
